About FindX

AI-Powered Lost & Found Platform – CSE Major Project

Reuniting People with Belongings

AI Driven Smart Matching System

FindX is an intelligent lost and found management platform designed to replace manual, inefficient search processes. By leveraging multi-factor algorithms, text tokenization, location proximity, and descriptor similarity, FindX automatically matches reported lost items with found items in real time.

7-Factor AI Algorithm

Analyzes text, TF-IDF cosine similarity, brand aliases, color ranges, date decay, and Haversine location distances.

Secure Claiming

Prevents false claims by requiring secret identifying marks, IMEI/serial numbers, and proof of purchase.

Admin Panel & Analytics

Comprehensive dashboard for managing users, approving claims, viewing match scores, and category controls.

Technology Stack
